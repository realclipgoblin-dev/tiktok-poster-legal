Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information My Poster ("the App") handles and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok account information you authorize through TikTok Login Kit: your basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar).
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, used only to publish videos to your account.
  • Videos and captions you choose to publish.
  • Post performance data (such as view and like counts) for videos published through the App, if you grant that permission.

2. How we use it

Information is used only to authenticate you with TikTok, publish the videos you select, and show you how your posts perform. We do not use it for advertising.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok, through its official API, when you publish them.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and data are stored on the device or server that runs the App, not on a shared service. Reasonable measures are taken to protect them, but no method of storage is completely secure.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to run the App. You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions; stored tokens then stop working and can be deleted on request.

6. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests can be raised on this site's GitHub issues page.
